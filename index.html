Writing a review

Each review allows up to 3,000 characters per field — text, photos, video, no fixed format.

retrospective

Something you've had a long time. One thorough review, written now, drawing on years of use — durability, quality, everything that only shows up over time.

"Had this hammock 8 years. Still holding — here's everything that's worn, and everything that hasn't."

journaling

Something recent, or in active use right now. Short, dated notes added as things happen, rather than one long write-up.

"Bought this hammock 15 days ago. Day 4: noticed a seam starting to fray already."

versioning

Comparing named states of the same thing — a later model set against an earlier one.

"This year's jacket lost the inner zip pocket the 2013 version had."

services

A business, or a person there

A service isn't something you own, so there's nothing to be retrospective about in the same way — but journaling works just as well here, sometimes better. A dispute with a business is often a sequence, not a single moment.

You can write a review about the business as a whole, and separately, about a specific person there — whether that's the owner or someone who works for them.

journaling — a dispute with a business "Day 1: charged twice for the same repair. Day 3: called, was told the owner would call back. Day 9: owner called, said he'd refund it. Day 14: never refunded. Day 20: refunded in full, no explanation."
example — a person there "The garage overcharged me on parts. Ask for Karim if you go — he's the one who actually knows what he's doing."
First name only. No photos, no last names. Enough to point someone in the right direction — not enough to identify or target an individual.
interaction

Ask advice

Whichever kind of review you're reading — a product, a business, or a person there — you can ask the reviewer who wrote it: for buying advice, or anything the review didn't cover. For now, that exchange stays between the two of you — it isn't opened up into a wider public thread.

review — running shoes, size 49.5, 8 months "Sole held up fine, toe box felt tight from day one."

reader asks "Same size — did you size up, or return them?"

reviewer replies "Sized up half a size. Still using them, 3 months on."
