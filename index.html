Writing a review

There's no length limit on an entry as a whole, and no fixed format — text, photos, video. It works the same whether you bought something two weeks ago or you've had it for twenty years, receipt long gone. That's the part that doesn't really exist elsewhere: most review sites assume you just bought the thing.

retrospective

Something you've had a long time. One thorough entry, written now, drawing on years of use — durability, quality, everything that only shows up over time.

"Had this hammock 8 years. Still holding — here's everything that's worn, and everything that hasn't."

journaling

Something recent, or in active use right now. Short, dated notes added as things happen, rather than one long write-up.

"Bought this hammock 15 days ago. Comfortable so far — the attachment straps feel flimsy though."

versioning

Comparing named states of the same thing — a later model set against an earlier one.

"This year's jacket lost the inner zip pocket the 2013 version had."

A business, or a person there

A garage, a shop, a relay counter usually isn't uniformly good or bad — it's rarely everyone's fault, or everyone's credit. You can write an entry about the business as a whole, and separately, about a specific person you dealt with there.

example "The garage overcharged me on parts. Ask for Karim if you go — he's the one who actually knows what he's doing."
First name only. No photos, no last names. Enough to point someone in the right direction — not enough to identify or target an individual.

Talking to a reviewer

Whichever kind of entry you're reading — a product, a business, or a specific person there — you can ask the reviewer who wrote it. For now, that exchange stays between the two of you; it isn't opened up into a wider public thread.

reader ↔ reviewer — no wider thread yet
entry — running shoes, size 49.5, 8 months "Sole held up fine, toe box felt tight from day one."

reader asks "Same size — did you size up, or return them?"

reviewer replies "Sized up half a size. Still using them, 3 months on."
